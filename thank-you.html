Worth thinking about before we speak

None of this is homework — it just means we can give you a straighter answer on the call.

  • What's bothering you most Eating, appearance, a denture that moves, or a tooth you know is on borrowed time.
  • Any medical history that matters Diabetes, blood thinners, bisphosphonates or a history of smoking all affect implant planning, so it helps to mention them early.
  • When you'd want to start It changes what we suggest — and whether spreading the cost over 12 months makes sense for you.
  • Anything you're nervous about Say it on the call. It is the single most useful thing you can tell us.
