Dr Triantafyllia (Rose) Kantzioura

Cosmetic treatment provider · Dentist · GDC No. 290358

I believe cosmetic dentistry should enhance your natural smile, not change who you are. That is the starting point for every case I plan.

I graduated from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, completed a postgraduate degree in Aesthetic and Restorative Dentistry, then moved to the UK to practise in Louth. In 2021 I began a double Master’s in Prosthodontics at King’s College London — the science of designing teeth that look right and last.

Composite bonding is one of my favourite treatments. It repairs chips, closes small gaps and improves symmetry while preserving your own tooth structure, and combined with professional whitening the difference it makes to how freely someone smiles can be considerable. Every makeover is planned around the individual, with aesthetics, function and long-term oral health weighed together.
