Cygnet House · Internal design reference

Motion lab — sections, cards and everything else

Twenty-seven patterns for the implants funnel, all live and all replayable. Every one is written against the same token names as index.html, animates only transform, opacity, clip-path or filter, and has a defined resting state — so nothing here depends on JavaScript to look right, only to know when to start.

Following your system setting.

S · Section entrances

How a band of the page announces itself as you scroll into it. Pick one and use it everywhere — a page where every section arrives differently reads as nervous, not rich.

C · Card choreography

Three cards entering together is a missed opportunity; entering in an order tells the visitor how to read them. Applies to the problem cards, the pricing row, the journey steps and the clinician tiles.

H · Headline reveals

Reserve these for the hero H1 and at most one section head. Used on every heading they stop being special and start being a tax on reading.

M · Media reveals

The funnel carries fourteen photographs. Giving them an entrance is the single biggest change available to how expensive the page feels.

I · Hover & interaction

These run on pointer, not on scroll — hover the demos rather than replaying them. On touch they simply never fire, which is why none of them hides information.

U · Micro-interactions

Small, specific, and each tied to one job on the page rather than applied as a theme.

What I would actually put on the funnel

Not all twenty-two. A landing page needs a small vocabulary used consistently — the goal is a page that feels considered, not one that performs. This set costs nothing in layout and reads as one system.
